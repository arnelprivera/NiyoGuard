ABOUT NiyoGuard

Technology That Protects What Matters.

NiyoGuard is an agricultural technology that focused on helping coconut farmers address rat infestations through practical, innovative, and sustainable IoT solutions.

Our first product, the NiyoGuard IoT Device, brings this to the farm: it detects potential rodent movement near coconut trees, discourages rodents with ultrasonic emitters, and keeps farmers informed, all on solar power.

Mission

To help coconut farmers reduce rat infestations through practical, sustainable IoT technology.

Vision

Coconut farms that are protected, productive, and managed with smart, sustainable technology.

Goals and Objectives

To deliver a reliable, solar-powered rodent deterrent that is easy to set up, works on the farm without grid power, and keeps farmers informed through Bluetooth and 4G SMS alerts.
